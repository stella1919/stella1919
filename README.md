## Selected work

### [Lumo｜旅光](https://github.com/stella1919/lumo-interaction-study)

A camera-based interactive AR travel experience with gesture-driven light effects, spatial drawing, and journey postcards.

基于摄像头的互动 AR 旅行体验：用身体动作、手势、空间绘画和生成式纪念卡，把一次互动变成可记忆的旅程。

**React · TypeScript · MediaPipe · Canvas · Vite**

### [Multimodal Cultural Evidence Explorer](https://github.com/stella1919/multimodal-cultural-evidence)

A reproducible workflow for exploring cultural-heritage records through images, text, embeddings, similarity structure, temporal change, and network views.

以图像、文本、嵌入表示、相似性结构、时间变化和网络视图，探索文化遗产记录之间的关系。

**Python · Streamlit · embeddings · visual analytics · reproducibility**

## Areas of focus

## Working principles

**Make it tangible.** Ideas become clearer when people can interact with them.  
**Keep the evidence visible.** Every visual result should remain traceable to its source.  
**Design for return.** A good prototype invites a second look, not just a first impression.

**让想法可体验。** 当人们可以直接互动时，复杂概念更容易被理解。  
**让证据可追溯。** 每个视觉结果都应能回到清晰的来源。  
**让体验值得再次进入。** 好的原型不只制造第一眼的惊喜，也支持持续探索。
